Good to know

Frequently asked

Why did Rohrman start giving back?

Giving back has been part of Rohrman Automotive Group since founder Bob Rohrman started the Bob Rohrman Automobile Dealerships Foundation in 1986. Rohrman Impact carries that legacy forward across all of the group's dealerships.

As CEO Ryan Rohrman puts it: "We believe that a thriving community lifts everyone up. It's not just about selling cars; it's about investing in the places we call home."

Who was Bob Rohrman, and what is his legacy?

Bob Rohrman built one of the Midwest's largest family-owned dealer groups over more than 50 years before he passed away in 2020. His giving left a lasting mark on Greater Lafayette and beyond: a $15 million lead gift that named Rohrman Field at Purdue's Ross-Ade Stadium, the multimillion-dollar Jefferson High School Performing Arts Center, a tennis complex for Lafayette Central Catholic, and decades of support for Meals on Wheels, Make-A-Wish, and local schools. In 2021 he received the AIADA Lifetime Achievement Award for that legacy.

How much does Rohrman give back each year?

In 2025, Rohrman Automotive Group gave $1,236,826 to more than 60 organizations across Indiana, Illinois, and Wisconsin. That included four signature campaigns: $92,950 to the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, $80,850 to animal shelters and rescues, $80,850 to childhood cancer causes, and $77,200 to the Indiana Donor Network.

How does the $50-per-new-car program work?

For each signature campaign, Rohrman donates $50 for every new vehicle sold across all of its dealerships that month, then presents the total to that cause. April supports blood cancer research, June supports animal shelters and rescues, September supports childhood cancer, and November supports the Indiana Donor Network in memory of donor hero Kaitlyn Isbell.

What causes does Rohrman support in Greater Lafayette?

In Greater Lafayette, Rohrman supports the Indiana Donor Network, the Humane Society for Greater Lafayette, IU Health Arnett Hospital, the Tyler Trent Foundation at Purdue, and Wabash College, among others. The group organizes its giving around five areas: volunteerism, education, athletics, cultural programs, and direct donations.

Does Rohrman give time as well as money?

Yes. Alongside the dollars, Rohrman employees gave more than 400 hands-on volunteer hours in 2025: packing 22,228 meals with Feed My Starving Children, assembling 545 Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, and refreshing homes and camp grounds across the communities the group serves.
