Four signature gifts

Group-wide, we set aside $50 for every new car sold across all 22 dealerships. Here is where it went.

April
$92,950

Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, our biggest single drive of the year.

June
$80,850

Animal shelters and rescues, including the Humane Society for Greater Lafayette.

September
$80,850

Childhood cancer, from Hope on Wheels to the Tyler Trent Foundation.

November
$77,200

Indiana Donor Network, in memory of donor hero Kaitlyn Isbell.

Tyler Trent Foundation

Tyler Trent's story started right here at Purdue, and his fight against cancer moved this whole community. We are proud to stand with the foundation carrying his name, funding cancer research and support for families walking the same road.
