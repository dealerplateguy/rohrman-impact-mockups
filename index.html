Two design directions

Two working mockups of the Rohrman Impact page, built from real photos, real press, and real giving data. Open each, scroll all the way through, and view them on your phone too. Then tell us which direction feels right.

Direction A

Storefront

A bold, modern "storefront" layout: a photo-led hero, swipeable card rows, and a scroll-driven giving meter. Busy and energetic, lots to explore.

  • Logo-forward hero over the team photo
  • Scrollable campaign tiles and photo shelf
  • Animated giving thermometer
  • Pinned charity map and news list

Direction B

Editorial

A calm, clean, editorial layout with warm tones and generous whitespace, modeled after the Finish Line site. Simple and confident, easy to scan.
